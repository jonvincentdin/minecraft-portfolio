## Continuity

Content follows normal document flow. Only the desktop project showcase may hold briefly. The next section is visible approaching from below; there are no scene replacements or portal/loading transitions between sections. Scrolling backward reverses visual transforms without changing content or firing actions.

## Previs scope

The video demonstrates this sequence with three featured sample projects, a complete project index, sample biography/skills, and a representative subset of experience/credential entries. Production must render every published record. The editor and live publishing are not simulated.

The original v1 demo screenshots are explicitly marked illustrative. The hero display uses an affine DOM image aligned with the existing renderer; it is not a new texture/PBR implementation. Dynamic shadows, glow, rigging and full model assembly are future proposals, not effects this video proves.
