# F06 — Experience characters

Route `#experience`. Five existing models and fixed category IDs: professional, student, casual, builder, explorer. Respectively: Work & collaboration, Education & coursework, Personal projects, Team projects, Learning & community.

Preserve stage, character selectors, title/intro, model rotate/reset/wireframe controls, timeline/chapters and comparison selection. Switching the active character updates detail content; selecting comparison checkboxes can show multiple chapters without changing their underlying data.

Records include character category, role/activity title, organization/context, start/end, description, contribution, evidence URL and sample flag. Editor may modify labels and records while retaining the five category IDs.

Acceptance: all five characters display two eyes, suitable outfit details and correct pointer rotation; compare at least two chapters; no stale 3D contexts after repeated switching. These are unrigged voxel assets with procedural idle motion, not full skeletal character animation.
